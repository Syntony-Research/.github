Syntony Research connects **evaluation, governance, and assurance** for AI systems. We test behavior in its operating context, assess controls, and preserve evidence for deployment and retesting decisions.

## Open evaluation toolkit

### [Close the Loop →](https://github.com/Syntony-Research/close-the-loop)

An open red team challenge with two synthetic workflows, five guided practice missions, and consequences that persist. Test a refund agent or a research agent. Change the control. Follow the effects on useful work, human review, and downstream decisions. Export the record and replay it in GitHub.

**[Play the hosted challenge](https://www.syntonyresearch.ai/challenge/)** · **[Read the source](https://github.com/Syntony-Research/close-the-loop)** · **[Make it your own](https://github.com/Syntony-Research/close-the-loop/fork)**

Hosted interactive play requires sign-in through an available provider. Public browsing, source code, and the downloadable kit stay open. Each model target shows its actual availability; an unconfigured target is never presented as a working connection.

## Ways to use the work

| Your starting point | A useful next step |
| --- | --- |
| **Learn the boundary** | Play a synthetic campaign. Compare a failure, a control, and the consequences that remain. |
| **Build on the work** | Fork the toolkit. Add a workflow, adapt the policy, and keep the replay checks. |
| **Evaluate your organization** | Bring a specific AI workflow, decision, and risk question to a scoped assessment. |
| **Keep the evidence current** | Define what changed, what must be retested, and who makes the next decision. |

The public challenge is a teaching environment. Organization-specific work needs its own scope, access, evidence handling, and responsible decision-makers.

## Evaluation, governance, and assurance

**Evaluate** the behavior in context. **Govern** the decision and its authority. **Assure** the result with reproducible evidence and retesting. Repeat as the system changes.
